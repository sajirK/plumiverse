🦉 Plumiverse

Learn by playing, at your own pace.

Learn by playing: reading, writing, maths, French, German, Spanish… Ages 3 to 14, no ads, no account, data stays on the device.

All games

  • 🇩🇪 German — Learn German: listen, recognise, read, build sentences and write, with spaced review.
  • 🇪🇸 Spanish — Learn Spanish: listen, recognise, read, build sentences and write, with spaced review.
  • 🇫🇷 French — Learn French: listen, recognise, read, build sentences and write, with spaced review.
  • 🎈 Colours & shapes — Recognise colours and shapes and count to 10, no reading needed.
  • ✏️ Writing — Trace lines, letters and numbers the right way, then write first words, then spell them without a model.
  • 🧮 Mental maths — Addition, subtraction, times tables, division, order of operations, negative numbers, fractions and percentages.
  • 🃏 Memory — Turn the cards over and find the pairs: memory and attention, from 3 to 9 pairs.
  • 🧩 Patterns — Find the rule and what comes next: picture patterns, then number sequences (+, −, ×2, squares, Fibonacci).
  • 🔍 Odd one out — Sort and compare: find the one that doesn't belong, with pictures then numbers (even, multiples, primes).
  • 🤖 Code: the little robot — Program a robot with arrows: sequences, the shortest program, then “repeat 3 times” loops.
  • 🕰️ What time is it? — Read an analogue clock: o'clock, half past, quarter past, quarter to, then set the hands yourself.
  • 🪙 The bakery — Pay the right price with coins and notes, give change, then work with cents.
  • 🫧 Bubbles — Pop bubbles for finger precision, then recognise colours and numbers.
  • 🐹 Whack-a-Mole — Reflexes and attention: catch the moles without touching the bats, then whack only the right numbers.
  • 🍉 Fruit slice — Slice the fruit with a quick swipe without touching the traps, then slice only the sums that make the right number.
  • ⚡ Lightning Reflex — Tap only what the rule asks: reflexes, attention and self-control, with rules that change.
  • 🦕 Dino Run — Run, jump at the right moment, collect apples and dodge snails all the way to the castle.
  • 🐠 Bubble race — Guide the fish with your finger, swim through the gaps and catch stars all the way to the big crab.
  • 🏰 The Magic Castle — Find your way through a maze: collect all the treasures, avoid the bats, find the magic door.
  • 🌍 Around the world — Each animal comes from one continent: place it on the world map to learn the continents.
  • 🌦️ Seasons and weather — Choose the right clothes for the weather, then recognise the four seasons and their order.
  • 🌱 My garden — A plant needs water and sun to grow (but not too much water): from seed to harvest.
  • 🩺 The animal doctor — Treat animals by sliding the right tool: caring, following steps and knowing the body.
  • 🍕 The pizzeria — Listen to the customer's order and make the right pizza: listening, memory and counting.
  • 🍦 The ice-cream shop — Listen to the order and make the right ice cream: cone or cup, flavours, sauce and toppings.
  • 🎵 Musical memory — Listen to a melody and play it back in the same order: auditory memory and attention.
  • 😊 My feelings — Recognise feelings on a face, understand how someone feels, and choose the kindest reaction.
  • 🌤️ My mood today — Say how you feel today, get ideas that help, and see your week of feelings.
  • 🎈 Breathe with me — Five guided deep breaths to calm down, with a balloon or a flower and a candle.
  • 🎨 Drawing — Draw freely with your finger: colours, a rainbow, stamps and an eraser.
  • 🎹 The funny piano — Play music freely on 8 big keys, with 4 funny instruments.
  • 🧱 The number tower — Build a tower with the right number of blocks, then add by completing the tower.
  • 📖 Reading (French) — Learn to read in French: first letters, spelling words, then building and reading syllables.
