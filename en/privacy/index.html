Privacy

  • Plumiverse creates no account and asks for no email address.
  • Children's profiles (first name, age, progress) stay only on the device (browser storage). They are never sent anywhere.
  • No ads or advertising trackers.
  • GoatCounter statistics: only if a parent turns them on in the parents' area. Plumiverse sends counts of app screens viewed, interface language at profile creation, games started, and completed levels (game, level, stars). Depending on its settings, GoatCounter may also provide aggregated browser, system, language, and approximate country trends. No name, profile ID, age, or individual play history is sent.
  • Parents can export or delete profile data from the parents' area.
